How to survive the hollow

Bring 2–8 players. The host starts each match. Touch another player to pass the bomb, then run! Every match starts with 3, 2, 1… HAUNT! The fuse never resets on a pass. Last ghoul standing wins. Eliminated players watch the rest of the match.

Collect candy for speed, a charm to block incoming tags for five seconds, or a portal for an instant teleport. A shield cannot stop your own bomb from exploding. Fog, witching-hour speed, and closing gates appear at random.

Joining during a match puts you in spectator mode. Choose “Play next match” between matches to take a spot. Wins and passes stay on this room's scoreboard until you leave.
