How to survive the hollow

Bring 2–8 players. The host starts each match. Touch another player to pass the pumpkin, then run! The fuse never resets on a pass. Last ghoul standing wins. Eliminated players watch the rest of the match.

Collect candy for speed, a charm to block incoming tags for five seconds, or a portal for an instant teleport. A shield cannot stop your own pumpkin from exploding. Fog, witching-hour speed, and closing gates appear at random.

Joining during a match puts you in spectator mode. Choose “Play next match” between matches to take a spot. Wins and passes stay on this room's scoreboard until you leave.
